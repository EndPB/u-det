# Role marker

This package is **AICD numeric external control only**. It is not the d-det
ABC mainline. Do not run GPU training from this directory. The project's A/B/C
mean binary detection, Human-inclusive family attribution, and four-way
creation-type detection; they do not mean AICD T1/T2/T3. The real mainline
requires task-centred semantic separation, provenance-backed labels, and
train-only AMMC-style views.
